## Local differences

- Reduced the fixed four-phase ceremony to a compact evidence loop.
- Replaced absolute and accusatory language with observable decision boundaries.
- Removed dependencies on other Superpowers skills.
- Removed examples that risk exposing environment values during diagnosis.
- Replaced the upstream three-fix threshold with the owner's approved two-attempt stopping rule.
- Preserved proportional testing and allowed direct repair when the cause is already established.
